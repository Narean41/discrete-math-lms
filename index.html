About Discrete Mathematics Learning Management System

This web-based Learning Management System is custom-engineered for undergraduate Computer Science and Information Technology students to provide rigorous conceptual foundations in Discrete Computational Structures.

Course Objectives:

  • Understand mathematical proofs, formal inference rules, and compound truth functions.
  • Apply Set Theory identities, partial ordering relations, and Lattice algebra to computer programs.
  • Master combinatorial enumeration, the Pigeonhole Principle, and recursive sequence analysis.
